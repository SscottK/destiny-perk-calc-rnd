The Perfect Vault

The smallest set of guns — and the exact perks to roll on each — that covers every trait combination in the game.

  • Reference

Combos are unordered: A+B is the same roll as B+A anywhere it appears. The rarest combinations are solved first (low POS, the number of guns that can roll them) on the most flexible guns (high GFS, the sum of POS across everything a gun can roll).
3×3 guns are filled first and absorb every combo they can reach; 1×1 guns only gap-fill the combos that exist on no 3×3 gun. Copies already in the vault are topped up before any duplicate is bought.
